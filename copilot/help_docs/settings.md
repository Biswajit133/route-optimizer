# Administration (settings)

## Tests / services price list (DOS)
link: /settings/customerdos
keywords: test, tests, service, dos, price list, test price, add test, package, rate
**Administration › Services** lists your tests and packages with prices. Add or edit tests there, or import
them from a sheet.

## Cities
link: /settings/cities
keywords: city, cities, add city, serving city
**Administration › Cities** - cities your lab serves.

## Service areas
link: /settings/areacode
keywords: area, area code, service area, zone
**Administration › Service Areas** - areas inside each city.

## ZIP codes / pincodes
link: /settings/pincode
keywords: pincode, zip, zip code, postal code, serviceable
**Administration › ZIP Codes** - pincodes mapped to areas. An address whose pincode is missing here may not
get a slot or phlebo.

## Laboratories
link: /settings/lab
keywords: lab, laboratory, add lab, default lab
**Administration › Laboratories** - labs that process your samples.

## Lab locations
link: /settings/location
keywords: lab location, collection centre, drop location, branch
**Administration › Lab Locations** - drop points / branches of each lab.

## Slots and scheduling
link: /settings/slot-setting
keywords: slot, time slot, scheduling, booking time, slot duration, max orders per slot
**Administration › Scheduling Settings** - slot timings, duration and capacity.

## Visit reasons (cancel / reschedule / not collected)
link: /settings/visit-reasons
keywords: reason, cancel reason, reschedule reason, not collected reason
**Administration › Visit Reasons** - the reason lists shown when cancelling, rescheduling or marking not collected.

## Coupons
link: /settings/coupons
keywords: coupon, discount, promo code, offer
**Administration › Coupons** - create discount coupons (shown when coupons are enabled for your account).

## Online booking link
link: /settings/online-booking
keywords: online booking, booking link, self booking, patient booking
**Administration › Online Booking** - the public link patients use to book by themselves.

## Notifications (SMS / email / WhatsApp)
